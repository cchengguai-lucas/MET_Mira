### Key changes
- Added a 方案确认 section and a 工艺工程师 project role for electrical requirements.
- Requesting confirmation locks the note and emails the parties. All required yes votes lift the gate into 研发中. The status does not move by itself.
- Unlocking lets the writers edit the note. Requesting confirmation again asks DeepSeek whether the text changed in substance. A material change, or a failed comparison, clears the votes and sends mail again.
- Existing stored templates gain the section in memory. Their saved workflows stay as they are.
